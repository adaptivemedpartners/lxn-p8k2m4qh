Wedding dinner menu

Ceremony dinner choices · not daily restaurant menus

▾

This is the ceremony dinner selection guide from Guadalupe’s Wedding Menu (Eng.) PDF — not the à-la-carte restaurant menus. Final choices go on the Wedding Form.

Selection guidelines
  • Set menu for all guests — one option per course (starter, main, dessert).
  • If two mains are chosen, they are served alternating (guests get one at random).
  • Events over 40 guests may select up to three options of a dish.
  • Contract confirms whether the menu is three or four courses.
  • Dietary needs (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergies): tell the resort in advance.
